Why Glass

Built
different.

01
Real Iridescence
Thin-film interference computed per-pixel. Authentic rainbow spectrum based on viewing angle.
02
Fresnel Glass
View-dependent opacity and reflectivity. Clear at center, opaque and colorful at edges.
03
Organic Morphing
Multi-octave simplex noise vertex displacement. Alive and breathing — never static.
04
Spring Physics
Every interaction uses cubic-bezier spring curves for satisfying, tactile feel.
05
Zero Dependencies
Pure CSS + vanilla JS components. WebGL for the hero blob only. Drop it anywhere.
06
WCAG Accessible
AA compliant contrast. Full keyboard nav. Screen reader friendly throughout.
